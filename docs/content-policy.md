# Content policy

A source row is a lead for research and is not proof by itself.

The site currently fetches `data/javidnams_data.json`, which is refreshed from
an upstream source. Importing a row does not mean it has been verified,
editorially approved, or cleared for republication. The separate
`data/approved.json` file is not currently the frontend's active data source.

A record must have a stable identifier and at least one traceable source before
it can be described as verified. Important facts should be cross-checked where
possible. Conflicting information must be marked as disputed rather than
silently reconciled.

Do not publish national identity numbers or fragments, private contact details,
exact residential addresses, or unnecessary information about relatives.

A portrait may be published only when the depicted person's identity, origin,
copyright owner, and publication basis have been reviewed. A publicly
accessible image URL, face match, filename, caption, or image-search result is
not identity proof or reuse permission. Do not use face recognition or infer
identity from visual similarity.

A correction or removal request must be reviewed promptly. Keep an internal
change record without exposing the requester's personal details.

Only individually reviewed records and rights-cleared images should be
described as approved. Until that review is complete, treat imported material
as unverified candidate data and do not imply that its publication rights
have been established. See `docs/data-rights-policy.md`.
