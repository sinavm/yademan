# Data, image, and third-party rights policy

## The code notice is not a data license

The repository's proprietary `LICENSE` notice covers only original software,
documentation, and design elements owned by the repository copyright holder.
It does not transfer ownership of names, facts, personal data, photographs,
image URLs, or third-party datasets. Those materials may have different
copyright, database, privacy, publicity, and other legal protections.

## Upstream source

The site currently reads `data/javidnams_data.json`. The scheduled workflow
can refresh this file from the URL supplied through the
`JAVIDNAM_SOURCE_URL` Actions secret or variable. The fact that the upstream
file is publicly accessible does not establish permission to republish its
contents or images.

Before treating a record or image as cleared for publication, retain a
reviewable record of:
- the original source and retrieval date;
- the identity verification basis for any portrait;
- the copyright owner and exact license or written permission;
- permitted uses, attribution, restrictions, and any expiry;
- the reviewer and approval date.

An image URL, search result, caption, or filename alone is not proof of
identity or reuse permission. Do not assume that a remote image host's
availability grants redistribution rights.

## Personal and sensitive information

Memorial records may concern identifiable people and grieving families.
Publish only information that is necessary, reliable, proportionate, and
supported by a documented lawful basis. Do not publish private contact
details, identity numbers, precise home addresses, or unnecessary information
about relatives. Treat age, location, and images with care.

Provide a prompt correction/removal review process. Preserve only the minimum
internal audit record needed to explain a decision, and do not expose a
requester's personal details.

## Review status

A row imported from an upstream source is not automatically verified,
editorially approved, or rights-cleared. Until a record and each associated
image have been reviewed, do not describe them as verified or licensed.
If the rights basis for a specific item is uncertain, do not republish it
until that uncertainty is resolved.

## Requests

For correction, removal, attribution, or licensing questions, contact the
repository owner through the contact options on the `sinavm` GitHub profile.
Include the relevant record or file path and enough source information to
locate the material; avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal data.
