# Image research protocol

## Status

Image discovery is in progress. Search results are leads only. No portrait is approved for publication by this project at this stage.

## Required checks for each candidate

1. Confirm the depicted person's identity using at least one reliable, independent source. A matching filename, caption, or search query is insufficient.
2. Record the original source page, photographer or rights holder, and the image's publication context.
3. Verify the exact license or obtain written permission. A visible image URL does not establish reuse rights.
4. Preserve a review record: reviewer, date, evidence, decision, and any restrictions.
5. Do not use face recognition or infer identity from visual similarity.

## Candidate status vocabulary

- `UNVERIFIED_NAME_SEARCH_ONLY`: returned by a name search; identity not established.
- `IDENTITY_CORROBORATED`: identity supported by reliable independent evidence; rights still pending.
- `RIGHTS_CLEARED`: identity and publication rights documented.
- `REJECTED`: wrong person, unreliable provenance, or unsuitable rights.

Only `RIGHTS_CLEARED` images may enter the public site, and only after editorial approval. Candidate search exports belong in private research storage, never in this public repository.
